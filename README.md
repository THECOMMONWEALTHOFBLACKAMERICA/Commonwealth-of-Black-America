# Commonwealth of Black America

**FROM OUR HISTORY, WE BUILD OUR FUTURE.**

The **Commonwealth of Black America (CBA)** is a voluntary civic, economic, cultural, and institution-building initiative created to organize Foundational Black Americans around democratic representation, economic development, historical preservation, education, community infrastructure, and long-term self-determination.

This repository serves as part of the Commonwealth's permanent public record.

---

## Mission

The Commonwealth exists to build durable institutions capable of strengthening the political, economic, educational, cultural, and community capacity of Foundational Black Americans.

Our approach is based on a simple principle:

> **Build → Demonstrate → Negotiate → Institutionalize**

The Commonwealth seeks to develop institutions that can operate lawfully today while building the organizational capacity necessary for greater community self-governance in the future.

---

## Foundational Citizenship

Foundational Citizenship is intended for individuals who can document direct ancestral lineage to Black American communities established in the United States before **1870**.

Historical records may identify qualifying ancestors using classifications including:

- Black
- Colored
- Mulatto
- Free Person of Color
- African
- African American
- comparable historical classifications

Citizenship is based upon documented genealogical descent rather than appearance or self-identification alone.

Supporting evidence may include census records, vital records, Freedmen's Bureau records, military records, emancipation records, court records, church records, land records, probate records, and other credible historical documentation.

## Government Framework

The Commonwealth's constitutional framework is organized around three principal branches:

### Executive

Responsible for administration, national strategy, foreign affairs, treasury functions, economic development, education, technology, infrastructure, public safety programs, and other executive responsibilities.

### People's Assembly

The representative legislative institution of the Commonwealth.

### Constitutional Court

Responsible for constitutional interpretation, internal disputes, election matters, citizenship matters, and other jurisdiction established by Commonwealth law.

---

## Progressive Ranked Democracy

The Commonwealth is developing a democratic system based upon ranked-choice voting and continuing public accountability.

Citizens may rank candidates in order of preference rather than being limited to a single binary choice.

The Commonwealth's governing philosophy holds that:

> **Government exists only while it continuously retains the confidence of Foundational Citizens.**

Public officials may therefore be evaluated not only during elections but through continuing standards of integrity, constitutional compliance, public service, transparency, attendance, leadership, and stewardship.

## C$ Monetary Development

The Commonwealth is researching a phased financial system known as **C$**.

The envisioned development path includes:

1. A regulated bank-backed digital wallet and payment network.
2. Integration with compliant digital-dollar or stablecoin infrastructure where appropriate.
3. Possible future development of an independently regulated monetary instrument if sufficient legal, financial, technological, and institutional capacity exists.

C$ is intended to function as infrastructure supporting the Commonwealth economy rather than as a substitute for the economy itself.

> **The currency is the rail. The economy is the power.**

---

## Public Safety

The Commonwealth envisions a professional **Community Safety Corps** capable of supporting communities through lawful activities such as:

- neighborhood presence
- community escorts
- conflict mediation
- missing-person assistance
- event safety
- youth intervention
- emergency coordination

Police powers such as arrest authority, warrant execution, compulsory enforcement, or access to government law-enforcement systems would only exist where specifically authorized by applicable law or governmental agreement.

---

## Commonwealth Tribunal

The Commonwealth Tribunal may initially provide:

- voluntary mediation
- consensual arbitration
- internal constitutional review
- citizenship disputes
- election disputes
- member and organizational disputes
- contract and business dispute resolution
- restorative justice programs

Any coercive governmental judicial authority would require appropriate legal authorization.
