# Commonwealth of Black America

**FROM OUR HISTORY, WE BUILD OUR FUTURE.**

The **Commonwealth of Black America (CBA)** is a voluntary civic, economic, cultural, and institution-building initiative created to organize Foundational Black Americans around democratic participation, economic development, historical preservation, education, community infrastructure, and long-term self-determination.

This repository serves as part of the Commonwealth's permanent public record.

## Mission

The Commonwealth exists to build durable institutions capable of strengthening the political, economic, educational, cultural, and community capacity of Foundational Black Americans.

> **Build → Demonstrate → Negotiate → Institutionalize**

The Commonwealth seeks to develop institutions that can operate lawfully today while building the organizational capacity necessary for greater community self-governance in the future.

## Foundational Citizenship

Foundational Citizenship is intended for individuals who can document direct ancestral lineage to Black American communities established in the United States before **1870**.

Historical records may identify qualifying ancestors using classifications including Black, Colored, Mulatto, Free Person of Color, African, African American, or comparable historical classifications.

Citizenship is based upon documented genealogical descent rather than appearance or self-identification alone. Supporting evidence may include census records, vital records, Freedmen's Bureau records, military records, emancipation records, court records, church records, land records, probate records, and other credible historical documentation.

## Government Framework

The Commonwealth's constitutional framework is organized around three principal branches:

- **Executive** — administration, strategy, treasury, economic development, education, technology, infrastructure, public safety programs, foreign affairs, and related executive functions.
- **People's Assembly** — the representative legislative institution of the Commonwealth.
- **Constitutional Court** — constitutional interpretation and internal matters assigned by Commonwealth law, including citizenship, elections, and member disputes.

## Progressive Ranked Democracy

The Commonwealth is developing a democratic system based upon ranked-choice voting and continuing public accountability.

> **Government exists only while it continuously retains the confidence of Foundational Citizens.**

## Economic Development

The Commonwealth seeks to develop interconnected institutions supporting entrepreneurship, merchant networks, community development, education, grocery stores and food cooperatives, food banks, community centers, housing initiatives, technology, investment, financial education, and capital formation.

## C$ Monetary Development

The Commonwealth is researching a phased financial system known as **C$**. The intended path begins with regulated bank-backed payment infrastructure, may later integrate compliant digital-dollar or stablecoin infrastructure, and contemplates independent issuance only if sufficient legal, financial, technological, and institutional capacity is established.

> **The currency is the rail. The economy is the power.**

## Community Institutions

Long-term Commonwealth development may include schools and educational programs, genealogy and historical archives, business-development networks, community centers, food-access systems, community safety programs, mediation and arbitration institutions, housing and development initiatives, technology infrastructure, and municipal or regional partnerships.

Where governmental authority is legally required, the Commonwealth's strategy is to pursue lawful agreements, contracts, legislation, partnerships, or delegated authority with existing governments.

## Public Safety

The Commonwealth envisions a professional **Community Safety Corps** supporting lawful activities such as neighborhood presence, community escorts, conflict mediation, missing-person assistance, event safety, youth intervention, and emergency coordination.

Police powers such as arrest authority, warrant execution, compulsory enforcement, or access to government law-enforcement systems would exist only where specifically authorized by applicable law or governmental agreement.

## Commonwealth Tribunal

The Commonwealth Tribunal may initially provide voluntary mediation, consensual arbitration, internal constitutional review, citizenship and election dispute resolution, member and organizational dispute resolution, contract and business dispute resolution, and restorative-justice programs.

Any coercive governmental judicial authority would require appropriate legal authorization.

## Transparency and Historical Record

This repository is intended to preserve a timestamped public record of the Commonwealth's development. Major constitutional revisions, institutional proposals, registry updates, and public reports may be preserved through Git commit history.

Private citizenship evidence and personally identifying information should never be committed to this public repository.

## Founding Reserve

The Commonwealth proposes that each Foundational Citizen contribute an initial **$1 Founding Reserve contribution**. Treasury policy and accounting for the reserve will be maintained separately from the public citizen registry.
