# Founding Citizen Registry

The **Commonwealth of Black America Founding Citizen Registry** is the public index of Foundational Citizenship records that have been approved for public disclosure.

The registry is designed to prove institutional continuity while protecting private genealogical and identity records.

## Public Record Fields

A normal public entry may include:

- registry number
- citizen name
- citizenship classification
- enrollment date
- public status
- brief eligibility statement

## Private Records

The public repository should not contain:

- birth certificates
- residential addresses
- signatures
- government identification numbers
- raw DNA data
- financial account information
- private information about living relatives
- full genealogical evidence files

Those materials belong in the protected citizenship archive administered under Commonwealth privacy and citizenship standards.

## Numbering Standard

Foundational Citizen public records use the format:

`CBA-FC-######`

Numbers are sequential and should never be reassigned to another person after issuance. If a citizenship record is later withdrawn, terminated, corrected, or superseded, the original number should remain in the historical register with the appropriate public status rather than being silently deleted.

## Founding-Stage Procedure

Until the Foundational Citizenship Commission is formally constituted, founding enrollments may be entered as provisional founding records pending Commission ratification.

After the Commission is operational, future entries should be published only after the required review process is completed or when a separate founding procedure expressly authorizes publication.
