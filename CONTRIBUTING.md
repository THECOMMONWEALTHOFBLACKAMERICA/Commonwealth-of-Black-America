# Contributing to the Commonwealth of Black America Public Repository

Thank you for contributing to the public institutional record of the Commonwealth of Black America (CBA).

## What belongs here

Public contributions may include:

- document corrections
- constitutional or policy proposals
- institutional-development proposals
- local chapter proposals
- economic-development ideas
- historical-source corrections
- transparency improvements
- software or data tools supporting public CBA functions

## What does not belong here

Do not submit:

- citizenship applications
- birth certificates or government IDs
- residential addresses
- signatures
- Social Security numbers or account numbers
- raw DNA files
- private information about living relatives
- confidential banking or financial information
- credentials, passwords, API keys, or authentication secrets
- material intended to facilitate illegal activity

If a citizenship or genealogy process requires private evidence, use the designated secure process rather than GitHub.

## Proposal format

A substantive proposal should explain:

1. **Problem** — what needs to change or be built?
2. **Proposed action** — what exactly should the Commonwealth do?
3. **Authority** — which constitutional article, code, office, or institution is relevant?
4. **Resources** — what people, money, technology, partnerships, or records are needed?
5. **Risks and safeguards** — what could go wrong and how should it be controlled?
6. **Implementation** — what are the first practical steps?

## Constitutional proposals

Clearly mark constitutional amendments as **PROPOSED**. A pull request or merge does not itself constitute constitutional ratification.

## Document corrections

For typographical, citation, formatting, or factual corrections, identify the exact file and section and provide the proposed corrected language or source.

## Public discussion

Disagreement is permitted. Threats, harassment, doxxing, fraud, impersonation, and publication of private records are not.

## Pull requests

Where pull requests are used, keep each PR focused on one topic when possible. The description should explain what changed and why.

By contributing, you acknowledge that public submissions, comments, and revision history may remain permanently visible in Git history even after later edits.
