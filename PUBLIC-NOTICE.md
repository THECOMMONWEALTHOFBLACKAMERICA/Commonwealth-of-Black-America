# Commonwealth of Black America — Public Notice

The Commonwealth of Black America (CBA) is presently organized through voluntary institutions and legal entities operating under applicable law.

Nothing published in this repository independently creates territorial jurisdiction, diplomatic immunity, exemption from applicable law, police authority, coercive judicial authority, tax-exempt status, banking authority, legal-tender status, or governmental powers that require authorization from an existing government.

Membership in the Commonwealth does not replace any person's existing citizenship, legal obligations, or duties under the laws that govern the place where that person resides or operates.

Where the Commonwealth seeks to exercise functions that require governmental authorization, its stated strategy is to pursue lawful recognition, legislation, contracts, intergovernmental agreements, municipal compacts, delegated authority, or other legally valid arrangements.

Nothing in this notice shall be construed as a renunciation of the Commonwealth's lawful aspiration toward greater political, economic, cultural, institutional, or diplomatic self-determination.

## Public Registry Privacy

Public citizenship-registry entries are designed to disclose only the information necessary to establish the historical record of enrollment. Private genealogy evidence, identification documents, signatures, DNA data, addresses, financial information, and private information concerning living relatives should not be published in this repository.

## Document Status

Materials in this repository may include founding drafts, proposed codes, policy frameworks, public registry records, and later adopted instruments. Individual documents should be read according to the status stated on the document itself.
