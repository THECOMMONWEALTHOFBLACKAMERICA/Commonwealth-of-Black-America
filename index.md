# Commonwealth of Black America

## FROM OUR HISTORY, WE BUILD OUR FUTURE.

The **Commonwealth of Black America (CBA)** is a voluntary civic, economic, cultural, and institution-building initiative centered on Foundational Black Americans and organized around documented lineage, democratic self-government, economic development, education, historical preservation, community infrastructure, and long-term lawful self-determination.

The Commonwealth is being built through an institution-first strategy:

> **Build → Demonstrate → Negotiate → Institutionalize**

## Founding Stage

The Commonwealth's public founding archive was established in August 2026. The current public working framework includes a Constitution — Vision Edition v2, Foundational Citizenship standards, Progressive Ranked Democracy, a Treasury and Founding Reserve framework, a phased C$ monetary strategy, local-government and municipal-compact planning, and an external-engagement policy.

## Foundational Citizenship

Foundational Citizenship is intended for individuals who can document direct ancestral lineage to Black American communities established in the United States before **1870**.

Eligibility is based on documented genealogy rather than appearance or unsupported self-identification. Private source records are not published in the public registry.

[Read the Foundational Citizenship Standard](citizenship/FOUNDATIONAL-CITIZENSHIP-STANDARD.md)

## Constitution and Government

The Commonwealth's public working Constitution establishes:

- an Executive Branch
- a People's Assembly
- a Constitutional Court
- a professional Civil Service
- a Bill of Rights
- Progressive Ranked Democracy
- continuous public accountability
- Treasury and reserve governance
- local and regional Commonwealth institutions
- municipal compact strategy
- justice, mediation, and arbitration institutions
- community safety and emergency-management planning
- external engagement and recognition policy

## Economic Development

The Commonwealth seeks to build interconnected economic institutions supporting businesses, merchant networks, education, food systems, housing and community development, technology, capital formation, and long-term productive capacity.

Its monetary strategy begins with regulated banking and payment infrastructure and contemplates later C$ development only through legally compliant stages.

[Read the Founding Reserve and C$ Framework](treasury/FOUNDING-RESERVE-AND-C-DOLLAR.md)

## Local Institution Strategy

The Commonwealth's local strategy is to build real membership, governance, programs, financial controls, and community capacity first. Where governmental authority is required, local institutions may later pursue lawful contracts, memoranda of understanding, municipal compacts, commissions, partnerships, or delegated authority.

[Read the Local Institutions and Municipal Compact Strategy](governance/LOCAL-INSTITUTIONS-AND-MUNICIPAL-COMPACTS.md)
