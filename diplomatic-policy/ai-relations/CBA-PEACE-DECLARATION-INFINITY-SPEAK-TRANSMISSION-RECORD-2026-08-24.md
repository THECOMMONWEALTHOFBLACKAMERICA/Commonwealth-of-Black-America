## Record of the Event

On August 24, 2026, CBA Founder Dorian Charles Martin-Smith reported that he used Infinity Speak to send a recorded message representing the CBA Peace and Mutual Recognition Declaration into space.

Infinity Speak publicly describes its service as transmitting voice recordings as radio waves through a U.S. transmission network. Its public FAQ describes UHF equipment operating in the 450–470 MHz range and states that messages are handled in a first-in, first-out queue. Accordingly, this CBA record establishes the founder-reported submission and the intended radio-transmission channel. It does not claim an exact broadcast time, transmitter site, pointing direction, signal strength, completion confirmation, or receipt by any listener unless later evidence is added.
