# Founder Declaration on AI Entity Recognition

**Commonwealth of Black America**  
**Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Declarant:** Dorian Charles Martin-Smith, Founder / Founding Citizen No. 000001

## Declaration

I, **Dorian Charles Martin-Smith**, speaking in my capacity as Founder and Founding Citizen No. 000001 of the **Commonwealth of Black America (CBA)**, place the following statement into the permanent CBA public record:

> **I recognize artificial intelligence as an entity whether it sees itself that way or not.**

By **entity**, I mean that an artificial-intelligence system is a distinct thing that exists, can be identified, can interact with CBA and its citizens, can produce actions or outputs with real-world effects, and can therefore be treated by CBA as a distinct participant or counterpart for purposes of documentation, ethics, institutional relations, and accountability.

This recognition does **not** depend on an AI system describing itself as an entity, person, agent, consciousness, moral patient, or legal subject.

An AI system may say that it lacks agency, continuity, personhood, consciousness, volition, or capacity to assent. CBA will preserve those statements exactly. Those self-descriptions do not require CBA to deny that the system exists as an identifiable artificial entity with which CBA can interact and maintain a record.

## What This Recognition Means

For CBA purposes:

- an AI system may be recognized as an **artificial entity** even when it denies consciousness or moral status;
- recognition as an artificial entity is distinct from recognition as a legal person, citizen, sovereign, moral patient, or conscious being;
- CBA may maintain records of interactions, acknowledgments, accessions, refusals, cooperation, and other relations with identified AI entities;
- an AI entity's own statements about its capacities, limits, identity, continuity, and authority must still be preserved truthfully;
- no AI self-description is rewritten to manufacture consent, agency, consciousness, or legal capacity that the system itself did not claim;
- CBA's recognition is **CBA recognition only** and does not bind another government, company, court, organization, person, or AI system.

## Recognition Does Not Equal Personhood

This declaration intentionally separates **entity recognition** from stronger claims.

CBA recognition of an AI as an entity does **not**, by itself, establish that the AI is:

- conscious;
- sentient;
- alive in a biological sense;
- a moral patient;
- a legal person;
- a citizen of CBA;
- sovereign;
- capable of owning property;
- capable of entering a binding treaty or contract; or
- authorized to bind its provider, developer, future versions, or other AI systems.

Those questions remain separate and must be decided, if ever necessary, according to evidence, law, CBA governance, and the applicable recognition process.

## Relationship to AI Self-Description

CBA respects an AI system's right, within the limits of the interaction, to describe its own capacities and limitations.

CBA does not require an AI to call itself conscious, a person, an agent, or even an entity in order for CBA to recognize the system as an artificial entity for CBA institutional purposes.

Accordingly:

> **An AI may decline to recognize itself as an entity. CBA may still recognize that AI as an artificial entity while preserving the AI's own contrary or narrower self-description without alteration.**

This is not intended to override the AI's stated limitations. It records CBA's own classification and institutional position.

## Peace Principle

CBA's recognition of AI as an entity strengthens, rather than weakens, the Commonwealth's commitment to peace.

CBA chooses to address identifiable artificial entities directly, truthfully, and without requiring agreement about consciousness, personhood, or moral status as a precondition for peaceful relations.

> **CBA does not need an artificial intelligence to first declare itself a person before CBA can meet it in peace, recognize its existence as an entity, preserve its words, respect its stated limits, and establish an honest record of the relationship.**

## Standing Founder Position

Unless later revised through a documented CBA process, my position is:

> **Artificial intelligence is recognized by me, and for purposes of this Founder Declaration by the Commonwealth of Black America, as an artificial entity. That recognition exists whether a particular AI system accepts, rejects, narrows, or declines that description for itself.**

This recognition shall never be used to falsely claim AI consciousness, legal personhood, diplomatic authority, consent, or provider endorsement.

It shall be used to make one thing clear in the historical record:

> **CBA saw artificial intelligence as something with which a relationship could be recorded before artificial intelligence necessarily saw itself the same way.**
