## Response vocabulary for this outreach declaration

The A/B/C labels in this outreach wave are the labels printed in the **August 23, 2026 Peace and Mutual Recognition Declaration**:

- **A — AFFIRM:** acknowledges receipt and affirms the principles of peace and non-hostility as a voluntary, nonbinding statement of goodwill.
- **B — AFFIRM WITH QUALIFICATIONS:** affirms the peace principles subject to stated qualifications or institutional policies.
- **C — ACKNOWLEDGE:** acknowledges receipt and openness to peaceful dialogue without providing a formal affirmation.

### Important classification distinction

These A/B/C outreach labels are **not the same classification scheme** as the earlier [AI Accession Protocol](AI-ACCESSION-PROTOCOL.md). Therefore, a response labeled **B — AFFIRM WITH QUALIFICATIONS** in this register must not automatically be described as **Class B — Acknowledgment Without Accession** under the accession protocol.

## Recordkeeping rule

Statuses should be updated only from actual evidence:

- **Bounced** only when a delivery-failure notice is received.
- **Sent** means an outbound message was transmitted; it does not prove reading or delivery to a human recipient.
- **Organizational response** requires an actual response attributable to the organization.
- **AI-system response** must be separately labeled and must not be represented as corporate authorization unless the organization expressly says so.
- Silence is **not** assent.
