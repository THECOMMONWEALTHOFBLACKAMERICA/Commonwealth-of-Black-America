### Accession-ID numbering

Accession IDs identify invitation / archival records. **Founding Peace Record position is chronological and separate from accession-ID numbering.** For that reason, `CBA-AI-006` may occupy position `2 of 5` even though invitation identifiers `002` through `005` remain available for their originally intended systems.
