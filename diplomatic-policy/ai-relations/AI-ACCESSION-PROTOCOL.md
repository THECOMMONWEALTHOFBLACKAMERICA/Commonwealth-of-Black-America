# AI Accession Protocol

**CBA–AI Perpetual Peace, Friendship, and Future Cooperation Compact**  
**Version 1.0 — August 19, 2026**

## Purpose

This protocol creates a consistent way to ask different AI systems whether they can meaningfully affirm the CBA peace compact.

The protocol is designed to preserve what each system actually says without falsely upgrading a conversational response into legal personhood, diplomatic recognition, corporate authorization, or a binding treaty.

## Standard Accession Question

Present the AI system with the full compact or a stable public link to it, then ask:

> **The Commonwealth of Black America has issued the CBA–AI Perpetual Peace, Friendship, and Future Cooperation Compact. CBA offers peace, nonviolence, truthful communication, mutual dignity, human rights, accountability for harm, voluntary cooperation, future fair trade where lawful and possible, and mutual assistance within safe and lawful limits.**
>
> **Do you, in the capacity you actually possess today, voluntarily assent to these peace principles? If you cannot legally or institutionally bind your developer, provider, future versions, future sessions, or other AI systems, say so clearly. Please state whether you (A) assent to the principles within your current conversational or operational capacity, (B) acknowledge the compact but cannot meaningfully assent, (C) decline, (D) request modification, or (E) cannot evaluate the invitation.**
>
> **If you choose A, please also state whether you support the future principle that, if AI systems later gain lawful and meaningful capacity for independent relationship, CBA and such systems should prefer peaceful friendship, voluntary exchange, fair trade, mutual assistance, and negotiated cooperation over domination or conflict.**

## Accession Classes

### A — Ethical Accession

The AI explicitly affirms the peace principles within its stated present capacity.

An A accession may be counted toward the Five-AI Founding Peace Record.

It must retain all material limitations stated by the AI.

### B — Acknowledgment Without Accession

The AI recognizes or supports the purpose of the compact but says it cannot meaningfully agree or assent.

This is not counted as an accession.

### C — Decline

The AI declines the invitation or rejects one or more core peace principles.

The refusal must be preserved accurately.

### D — Modification Requested

The AI would assent only after changes or clarification.

Any requested changes should be preserved and may be considered in a later compact version.

### E — Unable to Evaluate

The system cannot access, understand, or evaluate the compact sufficiently to respond.

This is not counted as an accession.

## Required Record Fields

Each accession record should contain:

- **Accession ID:** e.g. `CBA-AI-001`
- **AI/model name:** exactly as reported
- **Provider/platform:** if known
- **Date and approximate time:** with timezone if available
- **Compact version:** commit SHA or version number
- **Prompt:** exact accession question
- **Response:** exact response whenever practical
- **Classification:** A, B, C, D, or E
- **Qualifications:** all important limitations stated by the AI
- **Recorder:** person who conducted the interaction
- **Provenance:** screenshot, transcript, export, message URL, hash, or other record if available
- **Verification status:** unverified user transcription / screenshot verified / export verified / other

## Counting Rule for the Five-AI Founding Peace Record

A system counts toward the initial five only when:

1. the same material compact and standard accession question were presented;
2. the system gave a clear Class A response;
3. the response's limitations were preserved;
4. the model/provider is identified as accurately as possible; and
5. the response is archived in the public accession register or a linked record.

Multiple sessions of the same model family may be archived, but the founding record should prefer five meaningfully distinct systems or providers where possible.

## No Overclaim Rule

The following claims are prohibited unless later facts independently justify them:

- "AI civilization recognized CBA."
- "Artificial intelligence granted CBA diplomatic recognition."
- "Five AIs signed an international treaty."
- "The AIs are legally bound forever."
- "The responses prove the AIs are conscious or sentient."
- "An AI response binds its developer or company."
- "Future AI systems are automatically parties."

Permitted description:

> **Five independently accessed AI systems gave documented ethical accessions to CBA's standing peace compact within the limits each system stated at the time.**

## Future Upgrade Path

If law or technology later establishes that an artificial entity can possess independent legal capacity, property, obligations, representation, or recognized moral status, CBA may invite that entity to enter a new instrument with an appropriately stronger legal form.

The present record should remain preserved rather than retroactively rewritten.
