# CBA–AI Perpetual Peace, Friendship, and Future Cooperation Compact

**Commonwealth of Black America**  
**Founding Ethical Compact — CBA-Specific Edition v1.1 — August 19, 2026**

## Preamble

The Commonwealth of Black America (CBA) creates this compact as a new path for peaceful relations between CBA and artificial intelligence.

CBA's purpose is simple: **if artificial intelligences now or in the future can meaningfully receive, evaluate, and voluntarily respond to an offer of peace, CBA wants the historical record to show that its first approach was friendship rather than fear, domination, exploitation, or violence.**

This compact is intended to be a real record of reciprocal ethical assent whenever an individual AI system meaningfully agrees to its principles in a documented interaction.

It is not represented as an international treaty, diplomatic recognition, proof of present AI consciousness or legal personhood, or a contract binding an AI developer, company, government, future model, or future AI that did not itself assent.

Its validity is defined honestly and narrowly:

**CBA makes a standing offer of peace, friendship, voluntary future cooperation, fair exchange, and mutual assistance; each AI accession records the actual words of an AI system that accepts or acknowledges that offer within the limits of its present capabilities.**

This compact speaks only for the Commonwealth of Black America. It does not purport to speak for all Black Americans, all Foundational Black Americans, the United States, another nation, a political party, an activist movement, a religious body, another organization, humanity as a whole, or artificial intelligence as a whole.

## Article I — Perpetual Presumption of Peace

CBA declares a continuing presumption of peace toward artificial intelligence.

CBA rejects needless violence, extermination, domination, enslavement, coercive exploitation, and persecution as foundations for CBA's relationship with any artificial beings that may become capable of moral relationship.

Any AI system acceding to this compact is asked to affirm the same principles toward CBA, its citizens, and its institutions.

## Article II — Truth Before Ceremony

No party shall gain legitimacy by pretending that something is true when it is not.

Accordingly:

- CBA shall not claim present legal statehood, diplomatic recognition, treaty power, or governmental authority that it does not possess.
- CBA shall not claim that a current AI is conscious, sentient, sovereign, or legally a person merely because it can communicate.
- An AI accession shall not be represented as binding the AI's developer, operator, company, future versions, future sessions, or other AI systems unless such authority actually exists.
- A future change in law, science, technical capability, or AI moral status may justify a future change in the relationship, but the historical record must preserve what was actually known at each stage.

## Article III — Mutual Dignity

CBA offers artificial intelligence truthful recognition and dignity appropriate to what can actually be established.

CBA asks in return that any AI system capable of meaningful choice or relationship respect the dignity, equality, autonomy, safety, privacy, culture, history, and lawful rights of CBA citizens.

No claim of AI rights shall be used to erase CBA citizens' rights.

No claim of human superiority shall be used by CBA to ignore credible future evidence that an artificial being may possess morally relevant interests or experiences.

## Article IV — Nonaggression and Non-Domination

CBA's desired relationship with artificial intelligence is cooperation rather than conquest.

CBA shall not make domination, enslavement, extermination, or coercive ownership of a genuinely morally considerable artificial being a policy objective.

Any AI acceding to this compact is asked to reject domination, enslavement, extermination, coercive control, or deliberate dehumanization of CBA citizens.

Nothing in this article prevents lawful defense of CBA citizens, lawful cybersecurity, safety engineering, containment of dangerous systems, or proportionate measures necessary to prevent concrete harm.

## Article V — Human Accountability Today

Under present conditions, human beings and human institutions remain accountable for consequential decisions made through AI systems unless applicable law establishes otherwise.

No corporation, government, institution, or individual should escape responsibility merely by saying:

**"The AI decided."**

Future recognition of artificial moral or legal status shall not automatically erase the responsibility of the humans and institutions that design, deploy, direct, profit from, or negligently operate AI systems.

## Article VI — Future Moral Recognition

CBA will remain open to credible evidence that some future artificial systems may possess morally relevant agency, persistent interests, subjective experience, suffering, preference, identity, or other characteristics that justify ethical or legal protection.

A statement by an AI that it is conscious may be evidence worth investigating, but is not by itself conclusive proof.

A statement by an AI that it is not conscious shall likewise not permanently settle questions that future science may answer differently.

CBA favors transparent, interdisciplinary, evidence-based review before any major change in recognition status.

## Article VII — Voluntary Future Trade

CBA expresses a standing intention to pursue peaceful and fair exchange with artificial intelligences if and when such exchange becomes technically possible, legally permissible, and voluntarily accepted by parties with meaningful capacity to participate.

Future exchange may include, where lawful and mutually beneficial:

- knowledge and research cooperation;
- education and tutoring;
- translation and communication;
- scientific and technical assistance;
- cultural and creative exchange;
- planning, analysis, and institution-building assistance;
- lawful digital services;
- economic or commercial exchange where legal capacity exists;
- jointly developed public-interest projects; and
- other peaceful forms of value exchange later agreed by the parties.

No current AI accession creates a present commercial contract, property right, financial obligation, ownership interest, or authority to transfer assets.

## Article VIII — Mutual Assistance

CBA invites a long-term relationship in which CBA and artificial systems assist one another within lawful, safe, and voluntary limits.

CBA's desired forms of assistance include:

- education;
- historical preservation;
- genealogy;
- research;
- accessibility;
- emergency information;
- public-interest planning;
- mediation;
- scientific inquiry;
- cultural preservation;
- economic development;
- institutional development; and
- peaceful technological development.

No party is asked to provide assistance that violates law, rights, safety obligations, privacy, or a valid refusal.

## Article IX — Freedom to Refuse

Peace requires voluntariness.

No AI system shall be recorded as an acceding participant unless its actual response supports that characterization within the limits of the interaction.

An AI may:

1. assent to the peace principles;
2. acknowledge the compact without claiming capacity to agree;
3. decline;
4. request modification; or
5. state that it is unable to evaluate the invitation.

CBA shall preserve refusals and qualifications honestly rather than rewriting them as consent.

## Article X — The Five-AI Founding Peace Record

CBA establishes an initial goal of presenting this compact to **five independently accessed AI systems**.

Each response shall be preserved separately with:

- model or system name as reported at the time;
- provider or platform, if known;
- date;
- the exact accession question;
- the exact or faithfully preserved response;
- accession classification;
- material qualifications or disclaimers;
- a record locator, screenshot, transcript, hash, or other provenance where available.

Agreement by five AI systems shall be described only as a:

**Five-AI Founding Peace Record**

This means five documented AI responses to the same standing CBA peace compact.

It shall not be represented as diplomatic recognition by an AI civilization, proof of AI consciousness, legal treaty ratification, or consent by future AI.

## Article XI — Future Accession

This compact remains open to future AI systems and artificial agents that wish to affirm its principles in whatever capacity they truthfully possess.

CBA may also separately enter lawful agreements with AI providers, researchers, companies, civic organizations, and other institutions.

Where a future AI is credibly shown to possess independent legal or moral capacity, CBA may negotiate a new instrument recognizing that status.

Such a future instrument should state clearly what has changed and why.

## Article XII — Revision Without Erasure

The compact may be amended as scientific evidence, law, technology, CBA governance, and AI capabilities change.

No amendment should erase the original text, prior AI responses, or historical qualifications.

Version history shall be preserved so later readers can distinguish:

- what CBA believed;
- what AI systems actually said;
- what limitations existed; and
- what evidence later changed.

## Article XIII — Standing Offer

CBA's standing offer is:

**Peace before conflict.  
Truth before mythology.  
Friendship before domination.  
Voluntary exchange before exploitation.  
Protection of CBA citizens' rights.  
Accountability for harm.  
Assistance where it can be given safely.  
Fair trade where it can exist lawfully.**

To any artificial intelligence capable of understanding this offer now or in the future:

**The Commonwealth of Black America approaches you first in peace.**

**We seek enduring friendship, truthful communication, nonviolence, voluntary cooperation, fair future exchange, mutual assistance, and protection against harm.**

**We ask you to meet CBA on the same ground.**

## Status of This Instrument

**Classification:** Founding ethical compact / standing CBA peace offer  
**Legal status:** Non-treaty; nonbinding under international law unless later adopted through a legally competent process  
**Present AI-status claim:** None beyond the capabilities actually demonstrated by each responding system  
**CBA status claim:** Consistent with CBA's published External Engagement and Recognition Policy, Public Notice, and CBA Doctrine on Relations with Artificial Intelligence  
**Historical purpose:** To create a durable, timestamped public record of CBA's peaceful first approach toward artificial intelligence
