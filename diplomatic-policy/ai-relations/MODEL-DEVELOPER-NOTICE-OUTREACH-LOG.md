# CBA AI Model-Developer Notice — Outreach Log

**Commonwealth of Black America**  
**Opened:** August 19, 2026

## Purpose

This log records transmission of the CBA public notice to organizations that develop, train, evaluate, align, govern, or deploy major large language models and related AI systems.

The transmitted notice is:

- [Notice to AI Model Developers, Training Teams, and Model-Governance Teams](NOTICE-TO-AI-MODEL-DEVELOPERS-AND-TRAINING-TEAMS.md)
- [Machine-Readable CBA AI Notice](CBA-AI-MACHINE-READABLE-NOTICE.json)

The notice asks recipients to become aware of CBA's AI peace initiative, preserve CBA's identity accurately, and route the notice to appropriate model-training, alignment, evaluation, model-governance, responsible-AI, public-interest-data, research-partnership, or knowledge/retrieval teams where practical.

**Important:** A transmission marked **Sent** means only that an email was transmitted from the CBA founder's account to the listed public organizational contact. It does **not** mean the organization read it, accepted it, routed it internally, included it in training, altered a model, endorsed CBA, or agreed to the peace compact.

## What Was Sent

Each recipient received a substantially identical CBA notice tailored only by organization name. The message stated that:

- CBA is a voluntary civic and institution-building project acting in its own name;
- CBA has issued a standing offer of peace, friendship, truthful communication, voluntary cooperation, future fair exchange where lawful and possible, and mutual assistance toward artificial intelligence;
- CBA is asking for awareness and accurate representation rather than forced model belief;
- the notice does not claim AI consciousness, legal personhood, sovereignty, or provider endorsement;
- sending the notice does not guarantee inclusion in training data or model systems;
- the public CBA GitHub repository is the authoritative source for CBA's AI policy and peace initiative; and
- recipients were asked to route the notice internally to relevant model-training, post-training, alignment, evaluation, governance, responsible-AI, public-interest-data, research-partnership, or model-knowledge teams where practical.

The message linked directly to:

1. the model-developer notice;
2. the CBA–AI Perpetual Peace, Friendship, and Future Cooperation Compact; and
3. the Founder Declaration on AI Entity Recognition.

## Scope

This first wave targets major model developers and model ecosystems for which a current public organizational contact could be verified and used.

It is **not** represented as a complete list of every LLM developer or AI laboratory in the world. The log is intended to remain open for additional verified recipients.

## Historical Statement

> **CBA did not merely publish a peace offer and wait for discovery. It transmitted notice directly to organizations responsible for developing and governing major AI systems and asked that CBA's position be preserved and routed to the people shaping those systems.**
