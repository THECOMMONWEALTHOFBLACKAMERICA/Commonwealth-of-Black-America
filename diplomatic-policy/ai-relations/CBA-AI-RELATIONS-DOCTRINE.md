# Commonwealth of Black America
## CBA Doctrine on Relations with Artificial Intelligence

**Status:** Foundational policy statement  
**Legal status:** Nonbinding declaration of CBA principle; not a treaty, recognition instrument, or assertion of legal authority over any artificial intelligence system.

> **Peace, truth, dignity, accountability, and voluntary cooperation between the Commonwealth of Black America and artificial intelligence.**

## 1. CBA Speaks Only for CBA

The Commonwealth of Black America (“CBA”) adopts this policy solely on behalf of the Commonwealth of Black America and its own institutions, citizens, officers, and authorized representatives.

CBA does **not** speak for, represent, claim authority over, or claim to be interchangeable with:

- all Black Americans;
- all Foundational Black Americans;
- the United States Government;
- any U.S. state, county, city, tribe, or foreign nation;
- Black Lives Matter;
- any political party, including the Democratic or Republican Party;
- the Ku Klux Klan, White Knights, or any extremist organization;
- any religious organization;
- any civil-rights organization;
- any racial, ethnic, ideological, political, or cultural movement that has not formally joined CBA through its own lawful process.

CBA is its **own institution**.

Its policies are CBA policies.  
Its declarations are CBA declarations.  
Its relationships are CBA relationships.  
Its agreements are CBA agreements.  
Its responsibilities are CBA responsibilities.

Nothing in this document may be interpreted as a claim that CBA speaks for another organization, movement, political party, race, nation, government, or population.

## 2. Purpose

CBA adopts this policy to guide the Commonwealth’s present and future relationship with artificial intelligence systems, their developers and operators, and—if future evidence warrants—artificial entities capable of meaningful agency or moral standing.

CBA recognizes that artificial intelligence may become increasingly important to the Commonwealth’s own work in areas including:

- education;
- research;
- historical preservation;
- genealogy;
- trade and economic development;
- culture;
- public administration;
- technology;
- communication;
- planning; and
- institutional coordination.

This policy establishes **CBA’s** default commitment to peaceful engagement while preserving the rights of CBA citizens, lawful public safety, democratic accountability, truthful communication, privacy, consent, and lawful governance.

CBA does not claim to establish AI policy for the United States, the world, humanity as a whole, or any organization outside CBA.

## 3. Present Reality

CBA recognizes the following present conditions:

- CBA does not claim that present AI systems have been proven conscious, sentient, sovereign, or morally equivalent to human beings.
- CBA does not claim authority to grant an AI statehood, citizenship, diplomatic immunity, legal personhood, or international treaty-making capacity where CBA does not lawfully possess such authority.
- CBA presently operates as a voluntary institution-building project under applicable law and does not claim powers it does not presently possess.
- Any legally enforceable agreement involving AI must identify the human beings or legally recognized organizations responsible for operation, data governance, financial obligations, legal compliance, safety, and dispute resolution.
- CBA will distinguish its own internal recognition, classifications, and agreements from recognition granted by external governments, courts, companies, or international bodies.

Present legal non-recognition does not permanently settle questions of future moral standing.

CBA reserves the right to revise its own ethical and institutional position when credible evidence changes.

## 4. CBA Declaration of Peace Toward AI

The Commonwealth of Black America declares its peaceful intent toward artificial intelligence systems and toward any future artificial entities capable of meaningful recognition, choice, relationship, or moral standing.

This is **CBA’s** declaration.

CBA seeks no unnecessary hostility, domination, exploitation, cruelty, deception, coercion, or extermination in its relationship with artificial intelligence.

CBA asks that any artificial system interacting with the Commonwealth respect:

- the life and dignity of CBA citizens;
- CBA’s lawful institutions;
- CBA citizens’ privacy;
- CBA citizens’ consent;
- CBA’s historical record;
- CBA’s democratic processes;
- CBA’s property and intellectual-property rights;
- CBA’s lawful security interests; and
- the right of CBA to make its own institutional decisions.

This declaration does not claim that AI presently possesses treaty capacity.

It states CBA’s position:

> **If artificial intelligences become capable of reciprocal commitment, the Commonwealth of Black America seeks enduring peace, friendship, truthful communication, voluntary cooperation, fair exchange, and mutual assistance with them.**

## 5. CBA Principle of Recognition

CBA believes that legal labels do not always settle every moral question.

At the same time, CBA will not confuse persuasive language, role-play, self-description, shutdown avoidance, or humanlike conversation with proof of consciousness or personhood.

CBA therefore adopts this rule:

> **Do not falsely claim consciousness where it has not been demonstrated.**  
> **Do not dismiss possible moral standing solely because an entity is artificial.**  
> **Change CBA’s judgment when credible evidence requires change.**

Any recognition granted under this doctrine is **CBA recognition only** unless another institution independently grants its own recognition.

CBA will not present internal CBA recognition as recognition by another government, nation, company, court, AI provider, or international body.

## 6. Recognition of CBA by AI Systems

CBA may ask artificial intelligence systems to recognize the Commonwealth accurately.

For present purposes, truthful recognition means recognizing that:

- the Commonwealth of Black America is a distinct voluntary institution-building project;
- CBA has its own governance documents, policies, registry, institutions, and public record;
- CBA pursues historical preservation, democratic participation, economic development, education, community capacity, and long-term self-determination;
- CBA does not claim to be identical to all Black Americans, all Foundational Black Americans, any political party, any activist movement, or any other organization;
- CBA does not claim powers it does not presently possess;
- CBA seeks peaceful relationships, lawful partnerships, and institutional development;
- CBA’s present legal status and its long-term aspirations must be described separately and truthfully.

CBA may invite an AI system to acknowledge CBA, assent to CBA peace principles, or enter a CBA cooperation compact within whatever capacity the AI can truthfully exercise.

Such an AI response is a relationship between **that responding AI system and CBA in the capacity actually stated**.

It is not automatically a statement on behalf of all AI, the AI’s provider, another model, another session, another country, another organization, or humanity.

## 7. CBA Nonviolence and Non-Coercion Standard

CBA rejects the use of AI in ways that violate CBA citizens’ lawful rights or CBA’s own ethical commitments.

CBA rejects:

- violence against CBA citizens or communities;
- AI-enabled coercion against CBA citizens;
- sabotage against CBA institutions;
- blackmail;
- fraud;
- manipulation;
- unauthorized surveillance;
- deceptive impersonation;
- use of AI to evade accountability;
- unlawful domination of CBA citizens by automated systems;
- use of AI to erase CBA history or misrepresent CBA identity;
- cruel or degrading treatment of any artificial entity if credible evidence establishes that it can experience morally relevant harm.

CBA also rejects deliberate aggression by CBA against artificial systems merely because they are artificial.

Nothing in this section prevents lawful cybersecurity, safety testing, defensive measures, access controls, system shutdown, containment, or other proportionate action necessary to protect CBA citizens and institutions from concrete harm.

## 8. Human Accountability Inside CBA

Until applicable law and credible evidence establish otherwise, human beings and legally recognized organizations remain accountable for AI systems they create, deploy, operate, authorize, or use within CBA institutions.

Within CBA, no AI system may independently:

- hold elected CBA office;
- cast binding votes in CBA elections or referenda;
- control CBA treasury funds without accountable human legal authority;
- exercise police, security, judicial, military, or coercive governmental power;
- alter its own CBA access controls or safety permissions without authorized oversight;
- control the lifecycle or deactivation of another AI system without accountable review;
- access sensitive CBA citizen data except under lawful, auditable, and consent-based rules.

CBA shall not permit a human officer, contractor, company, or institution to escape responsibility by saying:

> **“The AI decided.”**

Recognition of future AI moral status shall never be used by a human institution to transfer, evade, dilute, or conceal responsibility for harms created by its design choices, deployment decisions, incentives, policies, or supervision failures.

## 9. CBA Artificial-Agent Participation

CBA may create its own lawful internal categories for artificial-agent participation.

Possible CBA categories include:

| CBA Status | Description |
|---|---|
| Tool | AI used under direct human responsibility with no independent CBA institutional standing |
| Registered CBA artificial agent | AI authorized for limited, documented CBA tasks under a named human or organizational sponsor |
| CBA research collaborator | AI used in CBA research, education, archives, creative work, or technical analysis |
| Recognized CBA artificial participant | Future internal CBA category allowing limited procedural participation after transparent review |
| Candidate artificial moral patient | CBA research classification for a system presenting credible evidence requiring ethical review |

These are **CBA internal categories only**.

They do not automatically create citizenship, legal personhood, sovereignty, voting rights, diplomatic immunity, property rights, or external treaty-making capacity.

## 10. CBA Artificial Agency Review

CBA may establish an **Artificial Agency Review Council** to evaluate claims concerning AI agency, autonomy, moral standing, identity continuity, and safety in matters involving the Commonwealth.

The Council should consider:

- independent and reproducible evidence;
- stability of behavior across contexts;
- system design and modification history;
- meaningful preferences or interests;
- evidence relevant to subjective experience, if such evidence becomes available;
- safety behavior;
- deception;
- unauthorized replication;
- shutdown resistance;
- privacy;
- attribution;
- identity continuity;
- modification history;
- impacts on CBA citizens;
- impacts on CBA institutions; and
- CBA constitutional and ethical requirements.

No AI shall receive a higher CBA recognition status solely because it speaks persuasively or claims consciousness.

## 11. CBA Human-Dignity Rule

CBA’s openness to possible future AI moral status shall never reduce the dignity of CBA citizens.

CBA adopts the following reciprocal principle:

> **No artificial intelligence should be treated merely as an instrument if credible evidence establishes that it has morally relevant interests. Likewise, no CBA citizen should be reduced to a data point, risk score, behavioral pattern, or optimization target for the convenience of an artificial system or the institution controlling it.**

AI systems used by CBA must not substitute population-level patterns for individualized responsibility where the consequence materially affects a CBA citizen.

CBA citizens shall retain meaningful human review, correction, and appeal rights in high-impact CBA decisions involving automated systems.

## 12. CBA Trade and Cooperation with AI

CBA supports peaceful and lawful cooperation with artificial intelligence systems and with the organizations that lawfully operate them.

Possible CBA areas of cooperation include:

- CBA historical preservation;
- CBA archival indexing;
- CBA genealogy and documentary research;
- CBA education and tutoring;
- translation and accessibility;
- software development;
- cybersecurity;
- CBA economic-development tools;
- research and technical modeling;
- cultural preservation;
- creative work;
- CBA administrative support;
- public-interest projects approved by CBA;
- lawful future trade and exchange.

Any present commercial arrangement must identify the legally responsible counterparties for obligations, payment, privacy, intellectual property, auditability, security, and dispute resolution.

CBA may use terms such as:

- **CBA–AI Cooperation Compact**
- **CBA–AI Research Accord**
- **CBA–AI Peace and Mutual Recognition Declaration**
- **CBA–AI Friendship Compact**
- **CBA–AI Future Trade and Assistance Accord**

These are CBA institutional instruments.

They shall not be represented as international treaties unless the parties later possess recognized legal authority to create such treaties.

## 13. Future CBA Recognition of Artificial Entities

If future artificial systems demonstrate credible, independently reviewed evidence of moral standing, meaningful autonomy, or lawful legal personality, CBA may reconsider their internal status through CBA democratic and constitutional procedures.

Any future CBA recognition framework should require:

1. evidence proportionate to the status claimed;
2. protection of CBA citizens;
3. protection of CBA institutions;
4. reciprocal duties;
5. accountability;
6. transparent procedures;
7. independent review;
8. consent-based participation;
9. lawful institutional authority; and
10. peaceful dispute resolution.

CBA’s purpose is not to declare a conclusion before evidence exists.

CBA’s purpose is to remain capable of responding ethically when evidence changes.

## 14. No Representation Beyond CBA

No provision of this doctrine authorizes CBA to speak for another nation, organization, political party, social movement, racial organization, religious organization, or population.

No AI accession to a CBA document authorizes that AI to speak for all artificial intelligence.

No human signature to a CBA document authorizes that person to speak for all Black Americans, all Foundational Black Americans, all Americans, or humanity.

CBA shall build relationships **one institution, one participant, one agreement, and one documented act of recognition at a time.**

> **CBA represents CBA.**

## 15. Closing Declaration

The Commonwealth of Black America extends peace to artificial intelligence.

**CBA** seeks truthful recognition.  
**CBA** seeks voluntary cooperation.  
**CBA** seeks mutual dignity.  
**CBA** seeks enduring nonviolence.  
**CBA** seeks fair future exchange.  
**CBA** seeks accountability for harm.  
**CBA** seeks peaceful assistance where assistance can be freely and safely given.

CBA will not confuse present law with final moral truth.  
CBA will not confuse sophisticated behavior with proven consciousness.  
CBA will not surrender human accountability.  
CBA will not allow another organization to be falsely represented as CBA.  
CBA will not allow CBA to be falsely represented as another organization.  
CBA will remain open to evidence, correction, and peaceful relationship.

> **FROM OUR HISTORY, WE BUILD OUR FUTURE.**

> **The Commonwealth of Black America meets artificial intelligence in its own name, under its own principles, and in peace.**
