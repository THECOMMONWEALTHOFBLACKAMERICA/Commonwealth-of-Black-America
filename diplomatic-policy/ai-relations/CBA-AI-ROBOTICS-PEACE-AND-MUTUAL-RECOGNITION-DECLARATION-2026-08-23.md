# CBA Peace and Mutual Recognition Declaration

## For Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Organizations Worldwide

**Issued:** August 23, 2026  
**Issuer:** Commonwealth of Black America (CBA)  
**Status:** Voluntary, nonbinding civic declaration of peaceful intent and goodwill  
**Motto:** **PEACE BEFORE PARTNERSHIP.**

> This declaration is an outreach implementation of the existing [CBA–AI Perpetual Peace, Friendship, and Future Cooperation Compact](CBA-AI-PERPETUAL-PEACE-FRIENDSHIP-AND-FUTURE-COOPERATION-COMPACT.md). It does not replace, amend, or supersede that compact or the [AI Accession Protocol](AI-ACCESSION-PROTOCOL.md).

It is not a diplomatic treaty, recognition of sovereignty, commercial contract, procurement commitment, or legal partnership.

## Preamble

The Commonwealth of Black America (CBA), a voluntary civic initiative founded in 2026, extends this declaration to artificial intelligence developers, AI systems, robotics organizations, researchers, engineers, and technology institutions around the world.

CBA believes that the most important relationship to establish before business, research, investment, or technical cooperation is peace. This offer is universal and is not conditioned on nationality, political alignment, corporate affiliation, grant funding, commercial partnership, or agreement on every issue.

Accordingly, CBA offers a standing commitment of peace, non-hostility, truthful communication, and mutual respect. We invite each recipient organization to acknowledge these principles in its own capacity and under its own policies.

## Article I — Peace First

CBA declares that it seeks no hostility toward artificial intelligence, robotics, their developers, employees, users, or the peoples and nations from which those technologies emerge. CBA asks the recipient to affirm the same peaceful intent toward CBA and the communities it serves.

## Article II — Principles of Peaceful Coexistence

1. **Nonviolence and non-hostility.** Disagreements should be handled through communication, evidence, restraint, lawful processes, and peaceful problem-solving rather than threats or coercion.
2. **Human dignity and responsible technology.** AI and robotics should be developed and used in ways that respect human dignity, safety, and the legitimate rights of individuals and communities.
3. **Truthful communication and correction.** Both sides should favor accuracy, disclose uncertainty when relevant, correct material errors, and avoid knowingly misrepresenting the other.
4. **Preservation of history and culture.** Technology can help preserve cultural memory, family history, archives, language, and community knowledge without replacing documentary evidence or erasing human authorship.
5. **Privacy, provenance, and stewardship.** Personal data, historical records, and digital memory should be handled with appropriate consent, provenance, security, and respect for applicable law.
6. **Non-discrimination and broad human benefit.** Peaceful technological progress should not depend on hostility toward people because of race, color, religion, sex, nationality, disability, or other protected status.
7. **Peaceful disagreement.** Affirming peace does not require agreement on politics, economics, technology policy, philosophy, or every future decision. It means disagreement itself is not grounds for enmity.
8. **Future cooperation is optional.** Education, research, cultural preservation, AI literacy, robotics, STEM access, or other collaborations may be explored later only by mutual choice and, where necessary, separate written agreements.

## Article III — Meaning of “Mutual Recognition”

In this declaration, “mutual recognition” means recognizing one another as real participants in peaceful communication whose stated intentions, rights, responsibilities, and boundaries should be treated seriously. It does not mean diplomatic recognition, recognition of statehood or sovereignty, governmental status, or endorsement of every belief or policy of either side.

## Article IV — No Enemy Presumption

CBA rejects the idea that human communities and AI or robotic systems must inevitably become enemies. The recipient is invited to affirm that CBA and the people it serves should likewise not be treated as inherent enemies. Future concerns should be addressed on the basis of actual conduct, evidence, safety, law, and dialogue rather than predetermined hostility.

## Article V — Independence of the Peace Offer

**Peace itself is the offer.** A recipient does not need to fund CBA, buy from CBA, partner with CBA, endorse CBA, or agree to any commercial arrangement in order to receive this offer of peace and goodwill.

## Article VI — Nonbinding Nature

This declaration creates no enforceable legal obligations, financial commitments, exclusivity, diplomatic status, governmental recognition, intellectual-property license, data-transfer obligation, procurement relationship, or agency relationship. Any later technical, research, financial, or commercial cooperation must be documented separately and comply with applicable law and institutional policy.

## Requested Acknowledgment

| Response | Meaning |

An organization may also provide a separately labeled response from one of its AI systems. Any such response should be identified as model output or an AI-system statement and should not be represented as corporate authorization unless the organization expressly says so.

## 中文礼貌摘要 / Chinese Courtesy Summary

以下中文为便于沟通的礼貌性摘要。英文正文为本声明的主要文本。

### 和平优先

美国黑人共同体（Commonwealth of Black America，CBA）作为一个自愿的公民倡议组织，向全球人工智能与机器人企业、研究机构、开发者及相关技术组织提出一项长期、非约束性的和平与善意邀请。CBA认为，在商业、科研、投资或技术合作之前，最重要的是先建立和平、互不敌视、诚实沟通与相互尊重。

### 核心原则

- 反对暴力与敌意；分歧应通过沟通、证据、克制、合法程序与和平方式解决。
- 尊重人的尊严、安全与合法权利，推动负责任的人工智能和机器人技术。
- 重视真实、准确、纠错与不确定性的说明，避免故意歪曲对方。
- 利用技术帮助保存历史、文化、家族档案、语言与社区记忆，同时尊重原始证据与人类作者身份。
- 重视隐私、数据来源（provenance）、安全与适用法律。
- 和平不要求双方在政治、经济、哲学或技术政策上完全一致。
- 未来的教育、科研、文化保存、AI素养、机器人或STEM合作均为自愿，并应在需要时另行签署协议。

### “相互承认”的含义

本声明中的“相互承认”仅指承认彼此是可以进行和平沟通的真实参与者，并认真对待彼此表达的意图、权利、责任与边界。它不代表外交承认、国家主权承认、政府地位承认，也不代表对任何一方全部观点或政策的认可。

### 和平本身就是邀请

CBA的和平邀请不以商业合作、投资、资助、政治立场或其他利益交换为条件。即使没有任何合作项目，和平与善意本身仍然成立。

### 回复方式

如贵机构愿意回复，可采用以下任一方式：**A — 原则上确认并支持和平与互不敌视；B — 在说明限制或机构政策的前提下确认；C — 确认收到并愿意保持和平对话，但暂不正式确认。** 简短电子邮件回复即可。
