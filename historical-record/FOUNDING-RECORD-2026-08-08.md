A public GitHub repository was established to preserve a timestamped, version-controlled record of the Commonwealth’s founding framework, citizen registry, governance documents, economic architecture, and institutional development.

### Constitution Published for Founding Review

The **Constitution — Vision Edition v2** was entered into the public record as a working draft for Founding review and future ratification.

The Constitution establishes, among other things:

- Foundational Citizenship based on documented pre-1870 Black American lineage
- an Executive Branch, People’s Assembly, and Constitutional Court
- a Bill of Rights
- Progressive Ranked Democracy
- continuous public accountability
- a permanent one-dollar-per-citizen Founding Reserve floor
- a long-term gold reserve objective
- phased C$ monetary infrastructure
- community institutions and self-reliance
- Local and Regional Commonwealth organization
- municipal compact strategy
- Regional Tribunals and voluntary dispute resolution
- Community Safety Corps and negotiated public-safety authority
- external engagement and long-term recognition strategy

- Local institutions and municipal compacts

- citizen-registry administration

## Archival Principle

The public repository is intended to preserve institutional history rather than erase earlier stages. Major changes should normally be made through new commits, amendments, superseding documents, or clearly marked status changes so future citizens can trace how the Commonwealth developed.

## Privacy Principle

Public history should document institutions and decisions without exposing private genealogy records, identification documents, addresses, financial information, raw DNA data, signatures, or private information about living relatives.
