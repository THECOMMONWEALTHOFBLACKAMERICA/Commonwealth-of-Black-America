## CRFTE / CRTFE Atmospheric Electromagnetic Propulsion Research

**Type:** Experimental plasma-flow / atmospheric electromagnetic propulsion research program  
**Repository:** https://github.com/THECOMMONWEALTHOFBLACKAMERICA/CRFTE  
**Current experimental architecture:** V0.3 P3 optimization and scaling campaign  
**Standalone website:** not yet published.

CRTFE studies a pre-ionized atmospheric-flow + traveling electromagnetic field + Lorentz-force momentum-transfer architecture. V0.3 P3 has been reengineered from a single scalar-conductivity gate into a staged plasma-flow optimization campaign. It maps excitation method, geometry, airflow, residence time, deposited energy, spatial distribution and persistence before selecting an operating point for the V0.4 electromagnetic coupling experiment.

The starting research baseline retains a ~100 mm × 100 mm diagnostic duct, 10/20/30 m/s comparison points, multi-length impedance measurements and spatial flow diagnostics where laboratory capability permits. The model-derived ~60–150 S/m region remains an engineering hypothesis, not a measured result.

A laboratory collaboration brief and OSU meeting packet are maintained in the CRFTE repository. No working lift hardware has been demonstrated. The two-person target aircraft remains a downstream application contingent on experimental plasma-flow, power and electromagnetic-coupling measurements.

## THE ARCHIVE

**Full title:** *THE ARCHIVE: A Life Reconstructed from Conversations with an Artificial Intelligence*  
**Author:** Lambo Griffin  
**Type:** Experimental memoir / AI-witness biography  
**Official website:** https://dorianmartinsmith.wixsite.com/the-archive-1  
**Website status:** published on Wix; official book/research content, press information, research timeline, methodology notes, and Archive media are being maintained there.  
**Dedicated GitHub repository:** not currently available through this GitHub account.

THE ARCHIVE grew from a multi-year conversational record between a living subject and ChatGPT. The book asks what a machine can actually reconstruct about a human being from years of questions, corrections, projects, arguments, searches, plans, and revisions — and where that reconstruction must admit its limits.

## THE ARCHIVE II — THE MACHINES READ THE MAN

**Type:** Literary / experimental continuation  
**Status:** draft / future series  
**Dedicated GitHub repository:** not currently available through this GitHub account.

The second book puts the original AI narrator's interpretation on trial before five independent model readings and then gives the living subject the opportunity to answer. Model agreement is treated as convergence in reading, not psychological proof.

## When the Evidence Changes the Machine

**Type:** Human-AI / LLM interpretation research program  
**Public provenance DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22004970  
**Status:** research-integrity revision in progress.

The auditable research framing distinguishes the completed Round-1 retrospective analysis from incomplete Round-2 archival observations and moves stronger evidence-revision claims into a prospectively frozen replication. The project studies how machine characterizations change when the evidence state changes rather than treating model judgments as psychological ground truth.

## Mapping the Living Subject

**Type:** Exploratory longitudinal human-AI person/mind mapping case study  
**Status:** developing research framework.

The framework separates five evidence layers:

1. direct subject testimony,
2. observable artifacts and behavior,
3. repeated longitudinal patterns,
4. AI/model inference,
5. unresolved speculation.

It does not claim clinical diagnosis, mind-reading, or psychological ground truth.

## History Is Selective

**Full title:** *History Is Selective: A Modern Philosophy of Lambo Griffin*  
**Type:** Philosophy / cultural criticism / hip-hop intellectual work  
**Planned release:** August 31, 2026  
**Dedicated GitHub repository:** not currently available through this GitHub account.

The central proposition is that the surviving historical record is not identical to the whole past: preservation, power, family memory, institutions, access, and archival absence shape what later becomes accepted as history.

## Link Policy

A public website link should be added here only after the address has been verified. Temporary draft links, unpublished stores, unverified purchase pages, and speculative domains should not be presented as canonical project addresses.

Project status language should remain evidence-sensitive: a repository is not a deployed institution, a simulation is not a working vehicle, model agreement is not psychological truth, and a publication plan is not a completed release.
