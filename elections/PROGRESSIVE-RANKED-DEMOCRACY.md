# Progressive Ranked Democracy

**Commonwealth of Black America**  
**Public Working Framework — August 8, 2026**

## Purpose

The Commonwealth’s electoral model is designed around preference, broad support, and continuing accountability rather than a single binary choice.

## Ranked Elections

Where practicable, voters rank candidates or parties in order of preference: first, second, third, and onward.

The Election Code shall establish transparent transfer, elimination, or tabulation rules designed to identify the candidate or choice with the broadest democratic support.

## One Citizen, One Vote

Each verified Foundational Citizen shall have one vote in each election for which that citizen is eligible.

Election systems should be:

- auditable
- accessible
- secure
- resistant to duplicate voting
- protective of ballot secrecy
- capable of producing a permanent public results record

## Continuous Public Confidence

Elections determine who takes office. Continuous public-confidence measurement provides an additional layer of accountability after an officer takes office.

The Commonwealth’s governing principle is:

> **Government exists only while it continuously retains the confidence of Foundational Citizens.**

A future Election Code may establish measures including integrity, constitutional compliance, economic stewardship, public service, transparency, leadership, attendance, and responsiveness.

Any rating-triggered recall or special-election process must include minimum participation thresholds, anti-manipulation safeguards, cooling-off periods, notice, due process, and independent review.

## Transparency

Final election totals, ranking distributions, public-confidence results, election audits, and adjudicated disputes should be preserved in a tamper-evident public archive while protecting the secrecy of individual ballots.

## Election Integrity

The Commonwealth intends to maintain an independent election-administration function responsible for voter eligibility, ballot integrity, audit procedures, identity verification, dispute records, and public reporting.

The Constitutional Court or a designated independent judicial body shall hear election appeals and challenges.

## Founding-Stage Elections

During the founding stage, no election should be represented as conferring governmental authority outside the Commonwealth’s voluntary institutions. Election results govern Commonwealth offices and internal institutions unless additional authority is later lawfully delegated or recognized.

---

**Status:** Public working framework. Detailed tabulation formulas, participation thresholds, recall rules, and election-administration procedures remain subject to a conforming Election Code and future ratification.
