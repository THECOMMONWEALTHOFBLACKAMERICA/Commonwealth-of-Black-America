# The Founding 100

The **Founding 100** is the Commonwealth of Black America's first major membership and institution-building milestone.

The objective is not simply to collect names. The Founding 100 should become the first durable body of verified Foundational Citizens, organizers, professionals, and community builders capable of turning the Commonwealth's founding documents into functioning institutions.

## What the Founding 100 are building

Initial priorities include:

- Foundational Citizenship verification procedures
- an interim Foundational Citizenship Commission
- constitutional ratification procedures
- ranked internal elections
- the People's Assembly and Executive institutions
- a Constitutional Court or interim judicial body
- Treasury bookkeeping and the Founding Reserve ledger
- local organizing circles and chapters
- business and merchant networks
- genealogy and historical-preservation systems
- education and community programs
- future lawful municipal partnerships

## Foundational eligibility

Foundational Citizenship is intended for people who can document direct ancestral lineage to Black American communities established in the United States before **1870**.

Applicants should eventually be able to establish a generation-by-generation lineage using credible records. Public GitHub Issues are **not** the place to submit those documents.

## Public participation versus private verification

People may publicly follow the project, contribute ideas, report document corrections, or propose institutions through the repository.

Private citizenship evidence should be submitted only through a secure citizenship process once that process is operational.

## First organizing sequence

### First 10

The first 10 serious organizers should help establish procedures, roles, meeting practices, citizenship review, Treasury controls, and the first recruitment infrastructure.

### First 25

At approximately 25 active participants, the Commonwealth should begin testing chapter procedures, committee work, public meetings, and division of institutional responsibilities.

### First 100

At 100 qualified and active Foundational Citizens, the Commonwealth should be positioned to move from a founder-led drafting stage toward a more formal democratic institution with ratification, elections, committees, public reporting, and multiple local organizing centers.

## Standard of participation

The Commonwealth seeks builders rather than spectators. Useful areas of contribution include genealogy, law, accounting, finance, education, technology, cybersecurity, public administration, business, construction, housing, food systems, public safety, mediation, history, communications, fundraising, and local organizing.

## Public conduct

Participation must remain peaceful and lawful. The Commonwealth does not authorize violence, threats, fraud, extortion, impersonation, unauthorized police activity, or the publication of private personal information.
