# Commonwealth of Black America — Public Roadmap

This roadmap tracks institutional development. It is a planning document, not a guarantee of timing or legal authority.

## Phase 0 — Founding Record

- [x] Establish public repository
- [x] Publish Constitution — Vision Edition v2 working draft
- [x] Publish Founding Citizen Registry structure
- [x] Record Founding Citizen No. 000001
- [x] Publish public status notice
- [x] Publish citizenship, election, Treasury, local-government, and external-engagement summaries
- [ ] Publish first tagged/released founding snapshot

## Phase 1 — Founding 10

- [ ] Recruit and document the first 10 serious organizers
- [ ] Adopt temporary founding procedures
- [ ] Establish secure private citizenship-application storage
- [ ] Form interim Foundational Citizenship Commission
- [ ] Establish public meeting calendar and minutes process
- [ ] Establish Treasury bookkeeping and Founding Reserve ledger
- [ ] Open official public contact channels

## Phase 2 — Founding 100

- [ ] Reach 100 verified or provisionally admitted Foundational Citizens under approved procedures
- [ ] Ratify or formally adopt the Constitution
- [ ] Conduct first ranked organizational elections
- [ ] Seat the People's Assembly and Executive leadership
- [ ] Constitute the Constitutional Court or interim judicial body
- [ ] Publish first annual public report
- [ ] Establish conflict-of-interest and ethics disclosures

## Phase 3 — Local Chapters

- [ ] Launch chapter standards
- [ ] Establish organizing circles in multiple cities
- [ ] Create local service and institution inventories
- [ ] Develop lawful community programs
- [ ] Create chapter financial-control standards
- [ ] Establish regional coordination where membership supports it

## Phase 4 — Economic Network

- [ ] Build participating-business directory
- [ ] Develop merchant and procurement network
- [ ] Establish Community Development Fund governance
- [ ] Develop CEI measurement methodology
- [ ] Evaluate regulated banking/payment partners for CBA Wallet
- [ ] Pilot lawful payment and member-account infrastructure

## Phase 5 — Community Institutions

- [ ] Education and tutoring programs
- [ ] Genealogy and historical archive
- [ ] Community centers
- [ ] Food-access and cooperative initiatives
- [ ] Business-formation and capital-access programs
- [ ] Mediation and consensual arbitration services
- [ ] Community Safety Corps pilot limited to lawful non-police functions

## Phase 6 — Municipal and Regional Compacts

After local institutions demonstrate membership, governance, operational capacity, financial controls, and public support:

- [ ] Identify candidate cities and public agencies
- [ ] Develop model municipal compact
- [ ] Seek MOUs, contracts, partnerships, referrals, or lawful delegated functions
- [ ] Pursue education, workforce, food-security, mediation, neighborhood-service, housing, cultural, emergency-response, and public-safety partnerships where appropriate
- [ ] Publish compact performance reports

## Phase 7 — Mature Financial Infrastructure

- [ ] Scale regulated CBA Wallet/payment rails
- [ ] Evaluate compliant third-party stablecoin integration
- [ ] Maintain strict reserve/development-capital separation
- [ ] Publish reserve and economic-network reporting
- [ ] Consider direct C$ issuance feasibility only after legal authority, capital, governance, compliance, cybersecurity, reserve, and audit requirements are satisfied

## Phase 8 — External Institutional Standing

- [ ] Expand cultural, academic, archival, economic, and diaspora relationships
- [ ] Develop observer and institutional partnerships
- [ ] Build documented membership and governance record
- [ ] Maintain professional foreign-affairs standards
- [ ] Pursue broader recognition or delegated authority only through peaceful and lawful mechanisms

---

**Strategy:** Build → Demonstrate → Negotiate → Institutionalize
