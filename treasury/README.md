# Commonwealth Treasury & C$ Documents

This directory contains the public working documents for the Commonwealth of Black America's Treasury, Founding Reserve, Commonwealth Dollar (C$), payment-network architecture, and economic measurement system.

## Core Documents

- [Founding Reserve and Commonwealth Dollar Framework](FOUNDING-RESERVE-AND-C-DOLLAR.md) - constitutional/public framework for the $1 Founding Reserve, reserve separation, C$ phases, and CEI.
- [Monetary & Economic System White Paper v1.1](MONETARY-AND-ECONOMIC-SYSTEM-WHITE-PAPER-v1.1.md) - detailed banking/program-partner review architecture, including funds flow, phase definitions, responsibility allocation, ten-year scenario assumptions, pilot readiness gates, transparency commitments, and legal/regulatory risk controls.

## Operating Doctrine

> **C$ does not create the Commonwealth's economic power. It is designed to make that power measurable, transferable, increasingly interconnected, and capable of supporting long-term institution-building.**

> **The currency is the rail. The economy is the power.**

## Separation Rule

The following are separate pools and must not be treated as interchangeable simply because they are part of the same Commonwealth ecosystem:

- customer payment balances;
- payment-stablecoin reserves, if any;
- the constitutional $1 Founding Reserve;
- Commonwealth operating funds;
- donor-restricted funds;
- Community / Commonwealth Development Fund assets.

## Present Status

The C$ architecture is developmental. Phase 1 is designed around regulated partner infrastructure and partner-held U.S.-dollar value. Direct C$ stablecoin issuance is a later-stage objective requiring the applicable external legal approval, reserve capacity, compliance systems, governance, cybersecurity, audit readiness, and operational scale.
