# Founding Public Statement

**Commonwealth of Black America**  
**August 8, 2026**

The Commonwealth of Black America is being organized as a voluntary civic, economic, cultural, and institution-building Commonwealth for Foundational Black Americans.

Its purpose is not merely to issue declarations. Its purpose is to build durable institutions.

The Commonwealth’s founding strategy is:

> **Build → Demonstrate → Negotiate → Institutionalize**

The Commonwealth intends to organize documented citizenship, democratic representation, economic cooperation, education, genealogy, historical preservation, technology, community infrastructure, food systems, business development, dispute resolution, public-safety programs, and professional external relationships.

Foundational Citizenship is based on documented direct ancestral lineage to Black American communities established in the United States before 1870.

The Commonwealth’s long-term goal is to increase the capacity of its citizens and communities to administer their own affairs through lawful institutions. Where governmental authority is required, the Commonwealth intends to pursue contracts, compacts, legislation, commissioning, delegation, recognition, or other lawful forms of partnership with existing governments and institutions.

The Commonwealth is also developing a phased economic architecture intended to strengthen internal economic circulation, capital formation, merchant networks, community development, and eventually a C$ financial system built on regulated and legally compliant infrastructure.

The public founding record begins with **CBA-FC-000001**, the first Foundational Citizen registry entry.

The Commonwealth’s Constitution — Vision Edition v2, public registry, Treasury framework, election framework, municipal compact strategy, and external-engagement framework are preserved in this repository as working founding documents for public review and future ratification.

This public repository is intended to create a transparent historical record. Private genealogy evidence, identification records, raw DNA information, addresses, financial information, and other sensitive records are intentionally excluded.

The Commonwealth’s founding principle is simple:

> **FROM OUR HISTORY, WE BUILD OUR FUTURE.**
