# Commonwealth of Black America — Public Participation Code

The Commonwealth of Black America welcomes serious, good-faith participation in its public institutional development.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- address ideas, documents, policies, evidence, and institutional proposals directly
- disagree without threats or harassment
- distinguish verified facts from proposals, interpretations, and speculation
- protect private citizenship, genealogy, financial, and identity information
- avoid impersonating Commonwealth officers or claiming authority not actually granted
- comply with applicable law and GitHub's platform rules

## Unacceptable conduct

The public repository may not be used for:

- threats or advocacy of unlawful violence
- harassment or targeted intimidation
- doxxing or publication of private personal records
- fraud, extortion, or deceptive fundraising
- unauthorized claims of police, judicial, diplomatic, or governmental authority
- credential theft, malware, or other malicious technical activity
- posting private citizenship applications, raw DNA data, account information, identity documents, or authentication secrets

## Debate and criticism

Strong criticism of Commonwealth policies, leaders, documents, strategy, history, economics, or constitutional design is not by itself misconduct. Public institutional development requires room for disagreement and correction.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit labels, close discussions, lock threads, hide or remove material where permitted by the platform, or take other reasonable steps to protect the repository from abuse, spam, privacy violations, or unlawful content.

Moderation authority over GitHub participation does not itself create constitutional authority outside the repository.
