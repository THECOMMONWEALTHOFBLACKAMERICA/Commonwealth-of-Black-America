# Founding Commission
## Commonwealth of Black America

**Established:** August 8, 2026  
**Authority:** Founding Resolution No. 001

The Founding Commission is the Commonwealth's temporary institution for organizing the founding period and preparing the transition to representative government under the Commonwealth's governing documents.

## Current Organization

**Convener / Founding Organizer:** Dorian Charles Martin-Smith  
**Additional Commission Seats:** Pending appointment and seating under adopted procedures.

The Convener may perform organizational and ministerial acts necessary to establish records, publish adopted founding instruments, receive applications, organize meetings, and prepare matters for Commission action. Matters requiring Commission ratification are not deemed ratified merely by action of the Convener.

## Initial Mandate

The Commission's founding-period responsibilities include citizenship review and ratification, registry governance, Founding 100 organization, treasury oversight, institutional records, election preparation, public administration, and lawful external relations.

## Institutional Principle

The Founding Commission is transitional. Its purpose is not to create permanent unelected authority, but to establish the conditions under which constitutional institutions and representative government can function.

## Public Record Rule

Commission appointments, resolutions, votes, and ratifications intended to have public institutional effect shall be entered into the Commonwealth's official public record. Sensitive citizenship evidence and protected personal records shall remain outside the public repository.
