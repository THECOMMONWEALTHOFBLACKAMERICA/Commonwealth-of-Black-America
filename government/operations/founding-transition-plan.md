# Founding Transition Plan
## Commonwealth of Black America

**Operational authority:** CBA-FR-001  
**Commencement:** August 8, 2026

This plan converts the Commonwealth's founding framework into an operating sequence while preserving the transition to representative government.

## Phase I — Institutional Record

- Publish Founding Resolution No. 001.
- Establish the Founding Commission public record.
- Maintain `CBA-FC-000001` as **Provisionally Enrolled — Commission Ratification Pending** until proper ratification.
- Maintain separate public and private citizenship records.
- Preserve the official governance package and version history.

## Phase II — Founding Commission

- Recruit qualified additional commissioners from the Founding 100.
- Record each appointment through an official instrument.
- Adopt Commission meeting, quorum, voting, recusal, and recordkeeping procedures consistent with the governing documents.
- Conduct independent review of provisional founding enrollments.

## Phase III — Founding 100

- Open structured expressions of interest.
- Verify eligibility under the Citizenship Code.
- Issue sequential provisional registry numbers only after required review.
- Build an initial pool of citizens capable of serving in administration, treasury oversight, elections, community infrastructure, law/policy, technology, economic development, and external relations.

## Phase IV — Treasury and Administration

- Establish compliant accounting infrastructure.
- Record only funds actually received.
- Maintain a ledger separating operating receipts, restricted funds, Founding Reserve receipts, and other authorized classifications.
- Continue regulated-partner exploration for the Commonwealth Economic Network and any future C$ payment functionality.

## Phase V — Public Institutional Presence

- Maintain the official public repository and public registry.
- Maintain a public institutional website with governing documents, registry access, Founding 100 information, official notices, and contact routes.
- Publish resolutions and material governance changes with dates and version history.

## Phase VI — External Relations

- Continue targeted outreach to legal, civic, cultural, economic, academic, philanthropic, municipal, and governmental contacts.
- Prioritize substantive meetings and institutional relationships over high-volume outreach.
- Present current legal and organizational status accurately while preserving the Commonwealth's long-term self-government and recognition objectives.

## Phase VII — First Election

- Establish the verified voter roll.
- Determine offices to be contested under the governing documents.
- Publish election calendar and candidate qualification rules.
- Conduct voter education and candidate qualification.
- Administer voting under the Election Code.
- Certify results and transfer appropriate authority from founding bodies to elected institutions.

## Immediate Execution Queue

1. Seat additional Founding Commissioners.
2. Ratify qualifying provisional citizen records through independent Commission action.
3. Expand the Founding 100.
4. Establish treasury/accounting infrastructure.
5. Strengthen the public institutional website and repository.
6. Convert exploratory outreach into scheduled institutional conversations.
7. Prepare the first-election implementation calendar once the required citizen base and Commission capacity exist.
