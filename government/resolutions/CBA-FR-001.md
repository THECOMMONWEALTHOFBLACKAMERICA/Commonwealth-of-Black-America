# COMMONWEALTH OF BLACK AMERICA
## FOUNDING RESOLUTION NO. 001
### ORGANIZATION OF THE FOUNDING COMMISSION AND COMMENCEMENT OF INSTITUTIONAL OPERATIONS

**Adopted:** August 8, 2026  
**Founding Organizer:** Dorian Charles Martin-Smith  
**Institution:** Commonwealth of Black America

---

### PREAMBLE

Whereas the Commonwealth of Black America has been organized as a durable civic-national institution for the advancement of Black American self-government, economic capacity, community infrastructure, representative institutions, cultural continuity, lawful partnerships, and the long-term development of broader institutional recognition;

Whereas a constitutional and administrative framework has been prepared, including the Constitution, Bylaws, Articles of Incorporation, Citizenship Code, Election Code, Treasury Charter, Foreign Affairs Manual, Diplomatic Protocol Manual, Identity Standards, and related public instruments;

Whereas the transition from founding organization to functioning institutions requires a temporary Founding Commission, a controlled citizen registry, accountable records, defined offices, and a lawful transition toward representative government;

Now, therefore, the Founding Organizer issues this Founding Resolution as the initial administrative instrument for commencement of Commonwealth institutional operations, subject at all times to the Constitution and subsequent lawful ratification, amendment, or supersession by the competent organs of the Commonwealth.

## ARTICLE I — FOUNDING COMMISSION

1. A temporary **Founding Commission of the Commonwealth of Black America** is hereby organized for the founding period.
2. The Commission shall consist of the Founding Organizer and additional qualified founding commissioners admitted under the governing documents and Commission procedures.
3. Until additional commissioners are seated, the Founding Organizer shall serve as **Convener of the Founding Commission** for organizational and ministerial purposes only.
4. The Convener shall not represent a provisional citizenship enrollment as Commission-ratified in the absence of the required independent Commission action.
5. The Commission shall terminate, reorganize, or transfer its powers as required by the Constitution and the installation of elected institutions.

## ARTICLE II — MANDATE

The Founding Commission is charged with:

1. safeguarding the constitutional and founding records;
2. administering provisional citizenship enrollment and subsequent ratification procedures;
3. maintaining the official citizen registry and protecting private citizenship evidence;
4. organizing the Founding 100 initiative;
5. establishing administrative offices necessary for institutional operations;
6. maintaining transparent treasury and accounting procedures;
7. preparing and supervising the transition to the first elections under the Election Code;
8. authorizing official public resolutions, notices, and institutional records;
9. developing lawful relationships with communities, municipalities, organizations, businesses, governments, and other institutions; and
10. preserving continuity between the founding period and the Commonwealth's future representative government.

## ARTICLE III — AUTHORITATIVE FOUNDING INSTRUMENTS

The public governance package maintained in the official Commonwealth repository shall serve as the authoritative working body of founding instruments, subject to their respective adoption, ratification, amendment, and supremacy provisions.

No administrative resolution may amend the Constitution by implication.

## ARTICLE IV — CITIZEN REGISTRY

1. The Commonwealth shall maintain separate **public** and **private** citizenship records.
2. Public registry entries shall contain only information approved for public institutional use.
3. Birth certificates, residential addresses, signatures, raw DNA, government identification numbers, financial account information, complete genealogical evidence chains, and other sensitive evidence shall not be placed in the public repository.
4. Pending formal ratification, the required public status shall be: **“Provisionally Enrolled — Commission Ratification Pending.”**
5. Founding Citizen Record `CBA-FC-000001`, assigned to Dorian Charles Martin-Smith, shall remain provisional until properly ratified.
6. No Founding Reserve contribution shall be recorded as paid unless funds have actually been received and entered into the appropriate financial record.

## ARTICLE V — ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICES

The following institutional functions are authorized for development during the founding period:

- Office of the Founding Commission;
- Office of Citizenship and Registry;
- Treasury and Economic Affairs;
- Electoral Administration;
- Foreign Affairs and Institutional Relations;
- Records and Publications; and
- such additional offices as may be lawfully established.

Authorization of an office does not by itself appoint an officeholder. Appointments shall be recorded separately.

## ARTICLE VI — TREASURY AND FOUNDING RESERVE

1. Treasury operations shall be governed by the Treasury Charter and applicable law.
2. Financial records shall distinguish received funds, pledged funds, restricted funds, operational funds, and any future reserve classifications.
3. The Founding Reserve shall not contain fictitious, assumed, or unreceived contributions.
4. The C$ economic program shall be developed only in the form authorized by the governing documents and applicable financial law; no public record shall falsely represent an internal credit, proposed payment instrument, or future monetary system as sovereign legal tender.

## ARTICLE VII — EXTERNAL RELATIONS

1. The Commonwealth may pursue lawful institutional relationships, memoranda, partnerships, cultural exchanges, economic initiatives, and discussions concerning its long-term political and civic objectives.
2. Commonwealth diplomatic titles, credentials, protocols, or documents shall not be represented as conferring diplomatic immunity, state recognition, treaty status, or privileges under international law unless such status is actually granted by a competent external authority.
3. External communications shall distinguish present institutional facts from long-term objectives.

## ARTICLE VIII — FOUNDING 100

The Founding Commission shall organize a **Founding 100** cohort to develop the initial citizen body, institutional leadership pool, and ratification base. Recruitment shall emphasize documented eligibility, public service, institutional competence, integrity, and commitment to the Commonwealth's constitutional order.

## ARTICLE IX — TRANSITION TO REPRESENTATIVE GOVERNMENT

The founding administration shall prepare a transition calendar that includes:

1. establishment of a verified voter roll;
2. completion of the required citizenship review and ratification procedures;
3. publication of election procedures and offices to be contested;
4. candidate qualification;
5. voter education;
6. administration of the first election under the Election Code; and
7. orderly transfer of authority from temporary founding bodies to elected institutions.

## ARTICLE X — PUBLICATION AND EFFECT

1. This Resolution shall be entered into the permanent public record as **CBA-FR-001**.
2. Publication authenticates the text as an official founding-period institutional record; it does not by itself create recognition by any state or government.
3. This Resolution takes effect upon publication by the Founding Organizer and remains subject to the Constitution and subsequent competent Commission action.
