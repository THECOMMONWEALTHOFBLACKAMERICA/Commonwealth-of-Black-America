# Commonwealth of Black America — Public Repository Privacy Policy

This repository is public. Information committed here may be copied, indexed, archived, mirrored, or preserved in Git history even after later edits.

## Public-by-design information

The repository may publish:

- constitutions, codes, charters, manuals, and policy drafts
- public meeting records and reports
- public officeholder information
- public registry numbers and status summaries
- approved public-facing institutional documents
- non-sensitive historical and genealogical summaries

## Information that must not be committed

Do not commit:

- Social Security numbers
- driver's-license, passport, military-ID, or state-ID numbers
- full birth certificates or other identity documents
- residential addresses unless deliberately published for an institutional office
- signatures
- bank account, routing, card, or payment credentials
- passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, recovery codes, or private keys
- raw DNA data or genetic-data files
- private medical information
- private information concerning living relatives
- citizenship evidence files containing unnecessary personally identifying information

## Citizenship and genealogy

Public registry records should use data minimization. The public record should establish that a citizenship determination exists without publishing the complete underlying evidence dossier.

Private applications, source images, vital records, DNA corroboration, and generation-by-generation evidence should be maintained in a separate access-controlled archive.

## Accidental disclosure

If sensitive information is accidentally committed, immediately stop further distribution, remove the information from the current branch, rotate any exposed credentials, and use GitHub's procedures for removing sensitive data from repository history where necessary.

Because Git is designed to preserve history, deleting a file in a later commit may not erase it from earlier commits.

## Public participation

People submitting Issues, Discussions, or pull requests should assume their usernames, comments, and submitted content will be public.

## Principle

**Publish the institutional record. Protect the evidence archive.**
